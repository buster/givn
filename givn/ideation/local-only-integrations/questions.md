| Q1 | What is the setting called in domain language and YAML shape, and does it default to "local only" for both new and existing projects? | confirmed | Confirmed 2026-09-23: the noun is `rules`; a flat scalar `rules: local-only` (default) or `rules: versioned` in `givn/config.yaml`. | Q2 | What does "the history is clean" mean exactly: not tracked at the current revision, detected-and-reported in past revisions, or rewritten? The boundary is reachable refs; unreachable objects, remotes, and forks stay outside the guarantee (G4). |

The strict local-only and clean-history guarantees apply to every other generated rule. |
